Granular Synthesis Simulator

Objective: Manipulate grain size, density, and window shape to transform a static audio recording into floating clouds or intermittent robotic effects.

Instructions:

  1. Click Start Simulator to generate the source audio buffer.
  2. Granular synthesis treats audio like sand. It plays hundreds of tiny pieces of sound called grains to build a texture.
  3. Follow the guided missions (1 to 4) to create a smooth, floating ambient pad (a sound cloud).
  4. Complete the final unguided challenge (Mission 5) to destroy the cloud and create a robotic glitch/stutter effect.
  5. Use the large button or your Spacebar / Enter key to trigger the granular engine.
